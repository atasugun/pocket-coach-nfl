# Pocket Coach

Pocket Coach is a web app that helps NFL coaches find mistakes in pass protection and pass rush, using player tracking data from the first eight weeks of the 2021 season. It sorts every error into three confidence levels: Level 1 (Confirmed) errors are recorded directly in the data, such as penalties and sacks allowed, and Level 2 (Likely) errors are caught by clear rules, such as an unblocked rusher or a stunt the line failed to pass off. Level 3 (Possible) errors come from two machine-learning models: one flags blocks that went far worse than expected for the situation, and the other flags the blocker nearest the rusher when the chance of QB pressure suddenly jumps. Every flag opens an animated replay with the player and the moment of the error highlighted, and recurring errors are grouped into ranked patterns so coaches can self-scout their own team or find weaknesses in an opponent.
